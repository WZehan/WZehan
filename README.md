## Current Projects

### Mobile Apps & AI

- 📚 **[WIT Library](https://wit-library.wangzehan.cn)** — My 2025 undergraduate capstone project, built with help from GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT. The Flutter app brings together library services in one cross-platform experience and is available through the App Store link on its product page.
- 🎵 **[BiliPod](https://bilipod.com)** — Built entirely with Codex during June–July 2026 as a vibe-coding experiment. It uses Flutter on the client, Supabase for authentication and data, and Resend for email verification. Distribution remains limited to beta testing because of potential copyright concerns.
